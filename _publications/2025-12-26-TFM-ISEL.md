### Abstract

Synchronous reluctance motors (SynRM) have very promising characteristics in the field of energy efficiency, because compared to induction motors, they do not have rotor currents and compared to synchronous motors they do not need permanent magnets.

The use of SynRM in variable speed applications, through electronic frequency converters, can achieve efficiency classes IE4 (Super Premium), while induction motors are in classes IE1 (Standard) and IE2 (High), and synchronous permanent magnet motors achieve classes IE3 (Premium Efficiency) and IE4 (Super Premium Efficiency), according to international energy directives.

Due to current electrical machine design techniques using the finite element method, electronic frequency converters, microprocessors and control techniques, SynRM motors have emerged in the field of high-performance variable speed drives.

The aim of this dissertation is to obtain the efficiency map of a SynRM through computer simulation using two-dimensional finite element analysis software. The dissertation also includes experimental tests to determine the SynRM efficiency map.
